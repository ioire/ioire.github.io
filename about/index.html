Irene Iodice

I am an Assistant Professor of Economics at City St George's, University of London and a CESifo Research Network Affiliate. My research lies at the intersection of international trade and industrial organisation, with a focus on how trade policy, regulations, and technical standards shape firms' sourcing decisions, production networks, and responses to globalisation.

My current research asks how rules of origin and technical standards reshape global sourcing networks, and when coordination across countries delivers gains that unilateral policy cannot.
