Data

  • PLAID
    Product-Level Indicators for International Trade
    Open database of product-level indicators for international trade for about 5,400 HS 6-digit products across all seven HS revisions (1992–2022). Indicators include Rauch classification, broad economic categories, perishability, hazardous materials, microchip content, and 3TG conflict minerals.
    Available as bulk CSV downloads, a JSON API, and an interactive explorer at plaid.julianhinz.com.
